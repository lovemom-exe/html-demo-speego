Preparing Your Shipment: Where Should You Start?

SpeeGo Editorial Team · September 17, 2026
Share: Facebook ↗ · LinkedIn ↗ · Copy link

When preparing an export or import shipment, having complete cargo information upfront ensures accurate pricing, minimizes transit delays, and eliminates unforeseen operational surcharges.

1. Cargo Details

Clearly define product names, HS codes (if applicable), and cargo characteristics (general goods, fragile items, liquids, or goods containing batteries/chemicals). Gross weight, dimensions, and volumetric cubic meters (CBM) dictate packaging requirements and freight rates.

2. Origin, Destination, and Timing

Specify exact pick-up locations (factory floor or loading port) and final drop-off destinations (destination warehouse, port of discharge, or fulfillment center). Establish a realistic Cargo Ready Date (CRD) so freight forwarders can secure vessel or air space in advance.

3. Choosing a Shipping Method

Depending on delivery urgency and budget, choose between ocean freight (Full Container Load FCL or Less than Container Load LCL), express air cargo, or international courier. Clear alignment on Incoterms (FOB, CIF, DDP) prevents disputes over cost division and risk transfer.

4. Confirm Before Shipping

Before dispatch, verify the Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (C/O), and compliant product labeling per destination country regulations. The SpeeGo operations team conducts comprehensive document pre-checks for seamless customs clearance.
