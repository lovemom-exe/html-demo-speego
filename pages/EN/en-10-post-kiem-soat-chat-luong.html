Quality Control at Every Stage: Ensuring Quality Before Shipment

SpeeGo Editorial Team · September 17, 2026
Share: Facebook ↗ · LinkedIn ↗ · Copy link

Quality Control (QC) is far more than a hasty inspection right before container loading. To eliminate defect risks and costly commercial disputes, professional QC protocols must be systematically executed at distinct milestones throughout manufacturing.

1. Pre-Production Inspection (PPI)

Audit raw materials, incoming components, and verified golden reference samples. Confirm factory management fully comprehends approved specifications, color fastness, material tolerances, and functional standards before mass manufacturing begins.

2. During Production Inspection (DUPRO)

Executed when 10% to 30% of order volume is completed. DUPRO identifies defects early in the assembly line, validates workmanship, and verifies actual output velocity against contract delivery schedules.

3. Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI)

Conducted when at least 80% of units are packed into master cartons. Inspectors apply international ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 (AQL) sampling tables to inspect functional performance, visual finishes, barcode scannability, and drop-test packaging durability.

4. Detailed Reporting and Decision Gate

Within 24 hours of inspection, SpeeGo QC specialists issue a comprehensive report featuring high-resolution defect imagery, functional test videos, AQL breakdown (Critical, Major, Minor), and unbiased pass/fail shipping recommendations.
