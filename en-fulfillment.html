FULFILLMENT WORKFLOW

Five steps. One US-standard workflow.

01

Barcode setup

Create or assign a barcode for each product. Barcode PDF files provided on request.

02

Inbound request (IR)

Create an inbound request in the OMS: pick SKUs and quantities. The OMS confirms and returns an IR code to print and attach to each package.

03

Receiving, inspection & put-away

Every shipment is inspected before put-away. Inventory updates automatically in the OMS in real time.

04

Fulfillment & shipping

Orders are picked, packed with video records, and shipped via USPS, UPS, or FedEx.

05

Returns handling

SpeeGo handles the full returns intake and processing workflow, free of charge on every order.

SERVICE POLICY

Fulfillment policy

Each case below is assessed from the order record, valid documentation, and the reconciliation between the customer, the SpeeGo warehouse, and the carrier.

01 Goods lost in the warehouse +

If warehouse inventory audits confirm goods are lost in storage, SpeeGo compensates based on the declared invoice value or actual purchase value verified with official documents.

02 Incorrect packing by the warehouse +

If the warehouse ships the wrong SKU or incorrect quantity due to picking/packing error (verified by video logs), SpeeGo covers all reshipment and return shipping costs.

03 Wrong label or delivery address +

In the event of label misprinting caused by SpeeGo systems, reprints and forwarding are free. If customer provided invalid address, we assist in rerouting per carrier policies.

04 Damage during packing or domestic transit +

SpeeGo supports filing insurance claims with carriers (USPS/UPS/FedEx) and coordinates prompt reimbursement for business owners.

05 Marked "Delivered" but not received +

Assistance with carrier carrier GPS delivery coordinates check and dispatching official location verification within 24–48 business hours.

06 Orders shipped later than the SLA +

For orders not dispatched by SpeeGo within the agreed dispatch SLA, 100% of the fulfillment handling fee for that order will be refunded.

FILING A CLAIM
